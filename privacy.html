Privacy Policy (English)

Daftar Al-Duyun (Debt Book) is an app for shop owners to record customer and supplier debts, payments and invoices. Contact: abedulrhman444@gmail.com.

Data on your device. Store, customer and supplier details, debts, payments, invoices and item photos you enter are stored on your device. We do not send them to our servers, sell them, or share them. A random device code is used only to activate the subscription.

Google Sign-In. If you sign in with Google, the app uses your email address to identify your account in the app. It requests the Google Drive (drive.file) permission only to save a backup of your data to your own Google Drive (a file named “دفتر الديون - نسخة احتياطية.json”) and to restore it on a new device. This permission only lets the app see files it created; it cannot see any other file in your account. The backup goes directly between your device and your Google account and never passes through our servers; the developer cannot read it.

Google API Services User Data Policy. Daftar Al-Duyun’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use this data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

Payments. You pay through a mobile wallet transfer and send the transfer photo yourself on WhatsApp. The app does not collect card or bank details.

Deleting your data. Uninstall the app or clear its data to remove data from the device; delete the backup file from Google Drive to remove the backup; revoke the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
